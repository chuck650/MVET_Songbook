📲 Progressive Web App (PWA) Installation

Installing the MVET Songbook on your home screen turns it into a standalone full-screen application. It hides web search bars, improves performance, and enables complete offline use!

🍏 iPhone & iPad

  1. Open the Songbook in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share icon (square with an up-pointing arrow at the bottom).
  3. Scroll down and select "Add to Home Screen".
  4. Confirm by tapping Add in Safari's header.

🤖 Android Devices

  1. Open the Songbook in Google Chrome.
  2. Tap the Three Dots menu in the top-right corner.
  3. Tap "Install App" (or "Add to Home Screen").
  4. Confirm by tapping Install.

💻 Windows (Chrome/Edge)

  1. Open the link in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click the Install Icon (a monitor with a down arrow) at the right edge of the top address bar.
  3. Click Install to place a stand-alone launcher shortcut on your desktop.

🍎 MacBooks (macOS Sonoma+)

  1. Open the link inside Safari.
  2. Click File in the top Mac menu bar.
  3. Select "Add to Dock...".
  4. Confirm the name and click Add to pin it to your desktop task dock.

🎼 Mastering the Interactive Rehearsal Suite

1

The Settings Drawer (⚙️) & Vocal Part Isolation

Access vocal controls by tapping the Gear (⚙️) icon in the top-right header tools. Under the Score Display section of the slide-out panel, use the Part Selection dropdown menu to switch from "Full Score" to your specific part (Soprano, Alto, Tenor, Bass, Men, or Women). The sheet music instantly re-renders to display only your selected vocal line, sweeping away the other staves so you can focus entirely on your part!

2

Score Zoom & Scale Calibration

Readability is key. Inside the Settings Drawer, use the Score Zoom range slider to scale the music notation from 30% up to 150% of its default size. Alternatively, you can calibrate default zoom levels globally from the main library view by clicking the gear icon and using the circular plus (+) and minus (-) default zoom buttons next to the zoom slider. The notation will dynamically re-wrap and scale perfectly on any tablet or mobile screen.

3

Rehearsal Track Player (FLAC & MP3)

Under the Rehearsal Tracks section of the settings drawer, choose your preferred high-fidelity audio format (FLAC or MP3). Tap play (▶) to listen to any vocal track, or download the audio files directly using the download (⬇) button. Playing a track slides up a premium Mini-Player Bar at the bottom of the screen, featuring an animated equalizer visualizer, Play/Pause control, timeline seeking slider, and an independent volume slider.

4

Synchronized Video Rehearsal

For visual learners, select a part's video rehearsal track inside the settings drawer and tap play (▶). This launches a synchronized high-definition Video Modal Overlay directly over the score viewer. You can practice in real-time with full standard media controls, and close (×) the modal at any time to return to the notation.

5

Performance Mode (Full-Screen Horizontal Flow)

Ready to perform? Tap the Fullscreen (⛶) button in the top-right header tools. This enters a gorgeous full-screen mode, sweeping away headers, controls, and drawers, and formatting your sheet music in a single continuous horizontal staffline. You can navigate measures by swiping/dragging, pressing keyboard arrow keys, or using the floating lower Navigation Pill's left/right arrow buttons.

6

Hands-Free Screen Wake Lock

To keep your hands free for holding your singing binder, the app automatically requests a background Screen Wake Lock whenever sheet music is open. Your device screen will remain awake and fully lit indefinitely without dimming, so you can focus entirely on singing without ever needing to tap your screen to keep it on.

🔄 Updating the App & Force-Refreshing

The songbook is designed to load instantly from your local cache. If a score has been updated or you notice visual display errors, you can force your device to download the latest files.

📱 Force-Updating Installed PWAs

If you installed the app to your Home Screen/Dock:

  • iPhone/iPad: Swipe the app away from your recent tasks window to force-close it, then reopen it while connected to the internet.
  • Android: Swipe the app away from your task list. If it still doesn't update, go to Settings -> Apps -> Songbook -> Storage -> Clear Cache.
  • Desktop: Click the three vertical dots icon in the app title bar, select Reload App, or close and reopen the app window.

💻 Desktop Browsers

Press the corresponding key combination while viewing the website:

  • Chrome, Edge, Firefox (Windows): Press Ctrl + F5 or Ctrl + Shift + R.
  • Safari (Mac): Press Option + Cmd + E to clear cache, then Cmd + R to reload.
  • Chrome/Firefox (Mac): Press Cmd + Shift + R.

📱 Mobile Browsers

If you are viewing the website inside a standard browser tab:

  • iPhone/iPad Safari: Go to iOS Settings -> Safari -> Advanced -> Website Data. Search for github.io and delete the storage entry.
  • Android Chrome: Tap the three-dot menu, select the "i" (Info) circle icon, tap "Site Settings", and select "Clear & Reset".
