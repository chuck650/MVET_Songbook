📲 Progressive Web App (PWA) Installation

Installing the MVET Songbook on your home screen turns it into a standalone full-screen application. It hides web search bars, improves performance, and enables complete offline use!

🍏 iPhone & iPad

  1. Open the Songbook in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share icon (square with an up-pointing arrow at the bottom).
  3. Scroll down and select "Add to Home Screen".
  4. Confirm by tapping Add in Safari's header.

🤖 Android Devices

  1. Open the Songbook in Google Chrome.
  2. Tap the Three Dots menu in the top-right corner.
  3. Tap "Install App" (or "Add to Home Screen").
  4. Confirm by tapping Install.

💻 Windows (Chrome/Edge)

  1. Open the link in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click the Install Icon (a monitor with a down arrow) at the right edge of the top address bar.
  3. Click Install to place a stand-alone launcher shortcut on your desktop.

🍎 MacBooks (macOS Sonoma+)

  1. Open the link inside Safari.
  2. Click File in the top Mac menu bar.
  3. Select "Add to Dock...".
  4. Confirm the name and click Add to pin it to your desktop task dock.

🎼 Mastering the Interactive Rehearsal Suite

1

Vocal Part Isolation (SATB Drawer)

Tap the controls bar at the bottom of any song score. You will see buttons for Soprano, Alto, Tenor, and Bass. Tap your part to highlight it, and drag the individual volume slider to solo your voice, or lower the other parts to Blending Mode to test your harmonies!

2

Display Zoom Calibration

Readability is key. Tap the floating plus (+) and minus (-) circles positioned at the bottom margin of the score viewer. This dynamically adjusts the size and layout of notation staffs on any tablet or mobile screen without distorting notation elements.

3

Hands-Free Screen Wake Lock

Our app automatically requests a background Screen Wake Lock when sheet music is open on your screen. Your mobile phone or tablet screen will remain awake and fully lit indefinitely without dimming, letting you rehearse hands-free with both hands on your binder!

4

Performance Mode (Full-Screen Live Layout)

Tap the Music Stand icon in the player controls. This enables our pure full-screen mode, sweeping away settings controls, buttons, and drawers. In this mode, the sheet music expands to full-width horizontal scroll with view-proportional symmetric margins, optimized for live chorus stands.

5

Sync Library for Offline Performance

No Wi-Fi at the rehearsal venue? No problem! Navigate to the Settings Gear icon from the library home view, and tap "Sync Library". The app will download and save all MusicXML files, high-fidelity isolated rehearsal MP3s, and printable PDFs directly to your device. You can then put your device on Airplane Mode and practice anywhere!
